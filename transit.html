Getting Around

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable, but we offer multiple ways to explore the rest of the island (Western Governors University, n.d.).

Public & Private Buses

Public buses serve Taniti City and run from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day. Private buses serve the rest of the island.

View Routes

Rental Cars & Taxis

Taxis are readily available in the city. You can also rent a car from a local agency near the airport.

Reserve a Car

Bicycle Rentals

Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors. Please note, helmets are required by law.
