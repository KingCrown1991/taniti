Frequently Asked Questions

Important information sourced directly from the Taniti Tourism Board (Western Governors University, n.d.).

Currency & Language

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar, but many businesses accept euros, yen, and major credit cards. Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English, though little English is spoken in rural areas.

Alcohol Laws

The drinking age on Taniti is 18 (not strictly enforced). However, alcohol is not allowed to be served or sold between the hours of midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Medical & Safety

There is one hospital with multilingual employees, and several clinics. Violent crime is rare, but tourists should be aware of pickpocketing. Power outlets are 120 volts (same as the US).
