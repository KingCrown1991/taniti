Dining & Groceries

Whether you want to eat out or cook your own meals, Taniti has plenty of options to satisfy your cravings.

Local & Global Restaurants

Taniti currently has 10 restaurants: five serve mostly local fish and rice, three serve American-style meals, and two serve Pan-Asian cuisine (Western Governors University, n.d.).

View Menus

Grocery Stores

Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store that is open 24 hours a day (Western Governors University, n.d.).
